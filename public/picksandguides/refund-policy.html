Refund Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Important: Picks & Guides is an independent editorial review website. We do NOT sell, ship, handle, or process payments for any products listed on our site. All orders, billing, shipping, and refunds are managed exclusively by the official product merchant you purchase from.

1. Who Handles Refunds?

When you click a product link on Picks & Guides and complete a purchase, you are buying directly from the official product merchant (the brand's own website). This means:

  • Your payment was processed by the merchant, not by Picks & Guides.
  • We have no access to your credit card, billing details, or order records.
  • We do not have the ability to issue refunds, partial refunds, or credits.
  • All refund requests must be submitted to the merchant directly.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund from an official merchant, follow these steps:

  1. Locate your order confirmation email sent immediately after purchase.
  2. Copy your order number or transaction ID.
  3. Visit the merchant's official website and go to the "Contact" or "Support" page.
  4. Contact customer support via email, phone, or support ticket.
  5. Provide your order number, full name, and email used at checkout.
  6. Follow any return instructions provided (such as returning unused/unopened bottles).

For most ClickBank-processed orders, you can also use the automated portal at: clkbank.com using the email and order number from your receipt.

4. Typical Refund Timeline

  • Most merchants acknowledge refund requests within 24–48 business hours.
  • If a physical return is required, allow 5–10 business days for processing after the merchant receives the package.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout.
  • Depending on your bank or card issuer, the refund may take 3–10 additional business days to reflect on your statement.

5. Common Reasons Refund Requests May Be Denied

  • Request submitted after the published refund window has expired.
  • Order was purchased from a third-party marketplace (Amazon, eBay, etc.) rather than the official site.
  • Unable to verify the order with valid order ID and matching purchaser email.
  • Evidence of misuse, fraud, or violation of the merchant's terms of service.

6. Affiliate Commissions and Refunds

When a purchase is fully refunded by the merchant, any affiliate commission Picks & Guides earned on the sale is automatically reversed by the affiliate network. We do not keep commissions on refunded orders.

7. Help with Refund Issues

If you have trouble reaching the merchant or locating your order details, you may contact us via our contact form. While we cannot issue refunds directly, we can help you locate the correct support channels and provide guidance on the typical refund process for products we've reviewed.

8. Changes to This Page

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time to reflect changes in merchant policies or our featured products. The "Last updated" date above indicates the most recent revision.
