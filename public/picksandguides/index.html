Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've answered the most common ones about shopping for supplements online.

Are these supplements safe to take?
Every product listed on Top Life Picks is manufactured in FDA-registered, GMP-certified facilities using generally recognized as safe (GRAS) ingredients. However, supplements affect people differently. Always consult your doctor before starting any new dietary supplement, especially if you take medication, have a pre-existing condition, are pregnant, or are nursing.
How long until I see results?
Most people report noticeable changes within 2–4 weeks of consistent daily use. For full effects, most manufacturers recommend a minimum of 90 days. Individual results vary based on diet, exercise, age, and overall health. We recommend purchasing a 3- or 6-bottle package for better pricing and to give the product a fair trial.
What if the product doesn't work for me?
Every product we feature comes with a minimum 60-day money-back guarantee (some have 90 days). If you're not satisfied, contact the official merchant's customer support and request a refund. Most companies refund even empty bottles — check the official site terms for details.
Do you own or sell these products?
No. Top Life Picks is an independent editorial publication. We do not manufacture, warehouse, or ship any products. All orders are processed directly by the official merchant. When you click a "Visit Official" button, you'll be taken to the brand's official website, where the transaction happens securely.
Why is there an FTC disclosure on your site?
The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) requires all affiliate websites in the United States to disclose their relationship with advertisers. When you purchase through links on our site, we may receive a small commission — this does not increase the price you pay and supports our independent research.
Is my credit card information safe?
Yes. All official merchants listed use 256-bit SSL encryption for checkout. Most accept Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, and PayPal. We never see or store your card details — all processing happens on the merchant's secure payment gateway.
Where are these products shipped from?
All products listed are shipped from warehouses within the United States (typically Florida, Utah, or Wyoming). Standard shipping is 3–5 business days inside the continental USA. Express options are usually available at checkout for an additional fee.
