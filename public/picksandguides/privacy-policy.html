Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Picks & Guides ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information about you when you visit our website (the "Service") and outlines your privacy rights. Picks & Guides is an independent editorial publication designed for residents of the United States.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Voluntarily Provide

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use our Service. If you contact us via the contact form or email, you may voluntarily provide:

  • Your name and email address
  • Any information you include in your message to us

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Service, we and our third-party service providers may automatically collect certain non-personally identifiable information. This data is used to understand traffic patterns and improve the quality of our reviews and rankings.

  • Log information (pages visited, entry/exit pages, date/time of visit, referring URLs)
  • Device information (browser type, operating system, screen resolution, device model)
  • General geographic location (country, state, or city-level only — no precise GPS)
  • Anonymous advertising identifiers and campaign attribution data (click IDs, keywords, etc.)

2. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies, pixel tags, and similar technologies solely for the following purposes:

  • Analytics: To understand which content is helpful and how visitors use the site (via Google Analytics and similar tools).
  • Advertising Attribution: To connect a referral from our website to a sale on an advertiser's website, in order to earn an affiliate commission. This is standard practice in affiliate marketing and is required to attribute the sale correctly. The FTC-compliant Affiliate Disclosure appears on every page.
  • Essential Functionality: Cookies required to remember basic preferences such as FAQ accordion state or mobile banner dismissal.

Most web browsers allow you to disable cookies through the settings menu. If you choose to disable cookies, some features of the Service may not function correctly.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information only for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and improve our editorial Service
  • To analyze traffic and trends using aggregated, de-identified analytics data
  • To respond to messages and support inquiries sent through our contact channels
  • To attribute qualifying purchases to our affiliate links so that we may earn commissions
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or technical issues
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use

4. Affiliate Advertising and Third-Party Disclosure

FTC Affiliate Disclosure: Picks & Guides is a participant in several affiliate advertising programs. When you click a product link on our site and make a purchase on a third-party merchant's website, we may receive a commission on qualifying sales at no additional cost to you. These commissions help fund our independent research and editorial operations. Commissions never influence how products are ranked or reviewed.

When you leave our website via a product link, your interaction is governed by the privacy policy of the destination website. We encourage you to review the privacy practices of every third-party website you visit, as we have no control over information you provide to them.

5. How We Share Your Information

We will never sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

  • With Service Providers: Vendors who perform tasks on our behalf (hosting, analytics, email delivery) and are contractually required to protect data.
  • Affiliate Advertising Partners: Only click attribution data (anonymous IDs, not your name or email) is passed through link redirects to properly credit qualifying sales.
  • As Required by Law: To comply with a subpoena, court order, or other legal process, or to protect our rights, users, or the public.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, information may be transferred as part of the transaction under equivalent privacy protections.

6. Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions and support correspondence for up to 24 months to resolve ongoing issues. Analytics and advertising attribution data is retained in de-identified or aggregated form for up to 26 months, in accordance with standard industry practices.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding personal information we hold about you:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request that inaccurate information be corrected.
  • Deletion: Request that personal information be deleted (subject to legal exceptions).
  • Opt-Out: Opt out of advertising cookies by adjusting your browser settings or using industry opt-out tools.
  • Do Not Track: Most modern browsers include a "Do Not Track" setting. There is no universal standard for honoring DNT signals, but our analytics providers respect major opt-out frameworks.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information in Section 9 below.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for or directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe we may have collected information from a child, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
